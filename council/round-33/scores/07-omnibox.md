## What is right
- The field cannot move. `plan()` (`src/omnibox.rs:150-160`) takes `top` from window height alone (`LIST_RESERVE: i32 = 359`) and only clamps `list` under it; `relayout`/`layout_with_column` re-place the block, not the anchor. Verified in pixels: the field's top border is at `y=191` in both `04-omni.png` and `04b-omni-picked.png` at `x=700`, and the list grows to `y=607` without touching it.
- The chip plus the path read as one address. `here_for_field` (`src/omnibox.rs:107-125`) takes the host out only when `site_of(authority)` matches the chip, `whole_address` (`:528-537`) puts a typed `/path` back on that site, and there are 7 unit tests pinning ports, users, IPv6 brackets and `about:blank` (`:560-660`). The shot shows `127.0.0.1` in 12px muted beside `/office` in 15px ink, `http://` nowhere.
- The picked row is unmistakable and the scrim is honest. `.suggest .sgrow.picked` gets `{wash}` plus a 2px `{ink}` left bar (`src/css.rs:194,198`); measured row fill `255,255,255` → `239,239,239` at `(900,333)` and bar `srgb(23,23,23)` at `x=587`, dark `26` → `58` with a `237` bar. No ghost: cropping `110x220+470+340` from `04-omni.png`, undoing the 0.88 card scrim and differencing against `01-default.png` gives `max 8, mean 0.03` of 255, and the "Start a project" pill at `(600,613)` reads `227`, which is exactly `0.12*17 + 0.88*255`, i.e. the modelled scrim and nothing else.
- Groups are labelled, ordered and capped so history cannot crowd out kept pages: heads come from a kind change (`src/omnibox.rs:377-384`), Open is capped at 3 (`src/app.rs:1330`), bookmarks at 3 (`kept >= 3`, `src/app.rs:1366`), the whole list at 8 (`offers.truncate(8)`, `src/app.rs:1397`), and the list scrolls inside `LIST_CAP: i32 = 420` rather than cropping. Escape (`src/app.rs:2204`), Return (`src/app.rs:2212` → `omnibox::commit`) and a press on the scrim (`src/app.rs:2227`) all exist.

## What costs points
- **No search row, ever.** Only three kinds are constructed: `kind: "Open"` (`src/app.rs:1344`), `"Bookmark"` (`:1361`), `"History"` (`:1380`). `src/omnibox.rs:407` still has a `"Search" => Icon::Search` arm and `src/css.rs:212` still styles `.sgkind`, so the branch is dead. Typing two words gives an empty list while Return still navigates (see `plain_target`, `src/app.rs:2555-2566`) - the list silently disagrees with the key. Costs about -1.0. Smallest fix: at the end of `fn offers`, when `typed` is non-empty and `plain_target(typed, ..)` is not a bare host, push one `Offer { title: format!("Search for {typed}"), url: resolve(...), kind: "Search" }` before the `truncate(8)`.
- **The chip can name a site the field is not on.** `site` is written once inside `show()` (`src/omnibox.rs:311-323`); the only other writer of the entry is `connect_changed` (`src/app.rs:2219`), which calls `refresh_offers` and never touches the chip. Type `example.com/x` over a `127.0.0.1` page and the field shows a `127.0.0.1` chip over another site's address - the exact lie `here_for_field` was written to prevent at load time (`:107-125`). Costs about -0.8. Smallest fix: in `connect_changed`, hide `site` when `chip_host(&plain_target(text, ..))` is empty or differs from the chip's current text.
- **Picking a tab leaves the field open over the new page.** `select()` (`src/app.rs:444-461`) calls `render()` and `schedule_session()` but never `close_omni` (grep: zero hits in that range), and the scrim only covers `column..width` (`src/omnibox.rs:271`), so the sidebar stays clickable. The field then stands over a page whose address it does not hold, with offers from the old tab. Costs about -0.6. Smallest fix: `if self.omni.visible.get() { self.close_omni(); }` in `select()` when `id != previous`.
- **Every row repeats the host the field just stopped repeating.** Each row prints `offer.url` in full (`src/omnibox.rs:420`), so `04-omni.png` shows `http://127.0.0.1:8099/titled?t=X` five times. The one thing that would distinguish them (the path) is the tail. Costs about -0.5. Smallest fix: put `here_for_field(&offer.url, &chip_host(&offer.url))` in the `.sgurl` label instead of `offer.url`.
- **No new-tab escape on a suggestion.** The row handler (`src/omnibox.rs:437-443`) closes and navigates the current tab for any button, with no modifier read, and `commit` has no Ctrl variant. Costs about -0.5. Smallest fix: read `SHIFT_MASK`/`CONTROL_MASK` in the row press and in `commit`, and call `new_tab(Some(&url), false, false)`.
- **No inline completion and no visible selection.** `open_omni` selects the whole address (`src/app.rs:1312-1318`, `select_region(0, -1)`), but the field interior `548x46+592+193` in `04-omni.png` is `24707` pixels of pure white with `0` non-grey pixels - nothing is drawn selected, so the address reads as text you must delete rather than text a keystroke replaces. Typing `examp` offers no completion of `example.com`. Costs about -0.4 combined.
- **Rows carry no accessible name.** `grep -c accessible src/omnibox.rs` is `0`; the rows are `EventBox` with `set_can_focus(true)` (`:389`) and a tooltip (`:390`), so the list is a pile of unnamed focusable boxes to ATK. Costs about -0.3.

## The three changes that would raise this score most
1. Add the `"Search"` offer in `fn offers` (`src/app.rs:1327-1400`) for any typed text that is not an address, so the list and Return agree instead of the list going empty. The `Icon::Search` arm and `.sgkind` rule already exist and are currently unreachable.
2. Make the chip follow the text: hide `site` in `connect_changed` (`src/app.rs:2219`) whenever the typed text resolves to a host other than the chip's, so the field can never show one site's name over another site's address.
3. Close the field when a tab is selected (`src/app.rs:444`), and let Ctrl+Return / Ctrl-click on a row open the suggestion in a new tab instead of replacing the page.
