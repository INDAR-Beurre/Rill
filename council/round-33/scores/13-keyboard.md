## What is right
- The panel is drawn from the same table the bench presses and the tests check: `build_shortcuts` (`src/panels.rs:846-869`) loops `for (_, key, what, ..) in crate::keys::SHEET`, and `must_be_advertised` (`src/keys.rs:341-360`) is an exhaustive match on `Intent` with no wildcard, so a new intent cannot ship undocumented. That is the right architecture, and it is why the sheet has 38 live rows instead of a hand-kept list that rots.
- Shift is handled correctly at the event boundary, not per key: `fn folded` (`src/keys.rs:78-80`) lowercases the keyval, and `shift_shortcuts_survive_the_upper_case_keyval` pins Ctrl+Shift T/R/B/H/U/I/C/N. `a_plain_ctrl_key_is_not_a_shift_key` pins the other direction (`Ctrl B` → `Nothing`).
- Escape is the one key that can belong to nobody: `taken` (`src/keys.rs:286-298`) returns false when nothing is open and the tab is not loading, and `Intent::Escape` in `apply` (`:219-233`) unwinds in the right order — omni, find, panel, reading, then stop the load.
- It is wired on all three surfaces a key can arrive at: the window (`src/app.rs:2245-2252`), the overlay (`:2492`) and the web view (`src/web.rs:388`), with `last_modifiers` recorded at `:2249` so find's Shift+Return works. F5 / Ctrl F5 split, `cycle` wrapping with modulo (`:1336-1347`), and the omni list scrolling to the picked row (`src/omnibox.rs:491-513`) are all correct.

## What costs points
- **Four rows — the last four, the ones a reader most wants — are painted outside the panel.** The card's bottom border is a single row at `y=779` (`srgb(148,148,146)` at `(900,779)` in `05-panel-shortcuts.png`, `srgb(110,110,110)` in the dark shot). Below it, at `x=560`, keycap chips continue at `y=762..794`, `803..835`, `836..868`, `869..901` and description text at `y=773-783`, `814-824`, `855-868`, `896-909` — "Reload", "F5 / Reload", "Ctrl F5 / Reload past the cache", "Ctrl Shift R / Reading mode (again to leave)" standing on the bare page with no card behind them. In light they are merely wrong (label pixels `min 23, mean 236` on white); in dark they are unreadable: the same band measures `min 237, mean 241.5` while the in-card band above it measures `min 26, mean 27.1`, because the label ink is `{ink}` for a dark card drawn on `srgb(252,252,252)` page. The leaked rows also lose the body's 15px inset — they start at `x=528` (the card's own left edge) where every in-card chip starts at `x=543`. The other five panels do not do this (`05-panel-settings.png` at `y=800` is the card's `236,236,236` shadow gradient and nothing else), so it is Shortcuts alone, because it is the only panel with 38 rows: `layout_card` clamps the card to `wanted_height.clamp(200,640)` (`src/panels.rs:190`) then `card_height.min(height-80).max(240)` (`:225`), and `Layer::place` (`src/layer.rs:26-29`) is `set_size_request` + `Fixed::move_` with nothing clipping the scrolled child to the card. The comment at `src/panels.rs:866-867` claims the sheet "scrolls like every other panel instead of losing its last rows"; the picture is the counter-example. Costs about -1.3. Smallest fix: give the scroll the height the card actually has — reuse the `above` sum already computed at `src/panels.rs:176-183` and `scroll.set_size_request(-1, (card_height - above).max(80))` before `layer.place` — and add a stress check that no ink exists between the card's bottom border and the window's bottom edge.
- **No focus ring, anywhere, and the panel never takes focus.** `grep ":focus\|focus-visible\|focus-within" src/css.rs` returns exactly one rule, `src/css.rs:266` `.omni entry:focus {{ border: none; box-shadow: none; }}` — a rule that *removes* one. Buttons, tab rows (`src/css.rs:215-220`), pins (`:209`), nav chips (`:305-307`) and the sheet's 38 rows have no visible focus state, so Tab through the chrome is invisible while `Tab` also means "the page's next field". And a panel never receives focus: the only `grab_focus` in `src/panels.rs` is the filter entry at `:143`, which Shortcuts does not build, and `open_panel_with` (`:148-193`) never calls `self.card.grab_focus()`, so `Ctrl /` opens a sheet whose rows cannot be reached, scrolled or read with the keyboard at all. Costs about -1.1. Smallest fix: `self.card.set_can_focus(true)` and `self.card.grab_focus()` at the end of `open_panel_with`, plus one `:focus` rule giving `{ink}` border / `{hover}` fill on `.panel button`, `.panel row`, `.row`, `.pin`.
- **The sheet scrolls nowhere visible.** 38 rows at a measured 41px pitch (`y=240, 281, 322, …` bands in both themes) is ≈1558px of content in a 553px body (`y≈226..779`), so 13 rows show and 25 are hidden, and the only affordance is a scrollbar that is not drawn: `scrollbar {{ background: transparent; }}`, `slider min-width: 6px` (`src/css.rs:333-336`), and columns `x=1186..1209` over `y=180..880` in both shots contain exactly one value each (`239` light, `26` dark) — zero slider pixels. Costs about -0.6. Smallest fix: a `.panel scrolledwindow scrollbar slider {{ background-color: {scroll}; min-width: 8px; }}` rule plus `set_overlay_scrolling(false)` in `body_scroll` (`src/panels.rs:289-295`) so the trough is real and always visible while the sheet has overflow.
- **The sheet advertises a distinction the code does not make.** `SHEET` lists `("closetab", "Ctrl W", "Close tab", "w", true, false, false)` at `src/keys.rs:165` and `("closetabshift", "Ctrl Shift W", "Close tab", "w", true, true, false)` at `:168`, while the intent arm is `("w", _) => Intent::CloseTab` (`:97`) — Shift is ignored, so the two rows are one press printed twice, under a file whose own comment says "the panel is wrong if it lists one way and not the other". `no_press_is_claimed_twice` misses it because it only rejects identical `(ctrl, shift, alt)` triples. There is also no window-close and no quit key at all (`grep quit` finds only the snapshot main loop at `src/app.rs:2222-2227`; `main_menu` at `:1888-1910` has no Quit and no accelerator labels). Costs about -0.4. Smallest fix: give Ctrl+Shift+W a real `Intent::CloseWindow` (or delete the row) and add `Ctrl Q` → quit, then extend `no_press_is_claimed_twice` to reject two rows whose `intent()` results are equal, not just their modifiers.
- **Find has no repeat key.** `F3`, `Ctrl G` and `next match` appear nowhere in `src/keys.rs` (`grep '"f3"\|"g", true\|find_next'` → 0 hits); the only way to step matches is Return inside the entry (`src/app.rs:2413-2421`) or the two arrow buttons in `src/find.rs`. Shift+Return (previous match) works through `last_modifiers` (`src/app.rs:2249`) and is not in `SHEET` at all, so the sheet's 38 rows omit the only navigation keys find actually has. Costs about -0.3. Smallest fix: add `("findnext", "F3", "Next match", "f3", false, false, false)` and `("findprev", "Shift F3", "Previous match", "f3", false, true, false)` to `SHEET` and map both in `intent()`.
- **Zoom and jumps are silent.** `zoom` (`src/app.rs:1801-1812`) clamps to `0.4..3.0` and paints nothing — no readout, no toast — while `copy_address` right beside it flashes `"Address copied"` (`:1328-1334`), so Ctrl +, Ctrl -, Ctrl 0 are unverifiable by eye until the page reflows, and Ctrl 0 on an already-100% page does nothing observable at all. Costs about -0.2. Smallest fix: `self.flash_toast(&format!("{}%", (view.zoom_level() * 100.0).round()))` at the end of `zoom` and `reset_zoom`.
- **The suggestion list is half-keyboard.** `step` clamps to `(-1, count-1)` (`src/omnibox.rs:475-486`), so Up from nothing does nothing and the pick stops dead at both ends with no wrap; the entry's handler (`src/app.rs:2361-2380`) knows only Up, Down and Escape — no Home, End, Page Up/Down, no Tab into the list, no Enter on a focused row (the rows are `set_can_focus(true)` at `src/omnibox.rs:389` but only `connect_button_press_event` at `:436` is wired). `Jump` is likewise silent past the end: `jump` (`src/app.rs:1349-1358`) special-cases 9 and otherwise `ids.get(n-1)`, a no-op with no feedback. Costs about -0.3. Smallest fix: wrap in `step` (`rem_euclid(count)`), handle `Page_Down`/`Page_Up`/`Home`/`End` in the entry handler, and flash a toast when `Jump` finds no tab.

## The three changes that would raise this score most
1. Stop the sheet escaping its card — size the scroll to `card_height - above` in `src/panels.rs:148-196` so no row can be laid out below `y=779`, in both themes — and make the overflow discoverable with a real slider in `body_scroll` (`src/panels.rs:289-295`, `src/css.rs:333-336`). Right now four of the 38 rows are painted on the bare page and, in dark, in ink that cannot be read.
2. Give the keyboard somewhere to stand: `set_can_focus(true)` + `grab_focus()` on the panel card in `open_panel_with` (`src/panels.rs:148`), and a `:focus` rule for `.panel button`, `.panel row`, `.row` and `.pin` (`src/css.rs` currently has exactly one `:focus` rule, and it removes a border), so Tab is visible and the sheet can be read without a mouse.
3. Make every advertised row a distinct act: replace the duplicate `Ctrl Shift W / Close tab` with a real window close (and add a quit key), add `F3`/`Shift F3` find-next/previous to `SHEET` and `intent()`, and extend `no_press_is_claimed_twice` in `src/keys.rs` to compare resolved `Intent`s rather than modifier triples so a sheet can never again print one action twice.
