## What is right

- The copy is unusually honest about mechanism and cost. `Use less memory` says "Turns off the one graphics path the engine can do without… Applies the next time the browser opens", and that is exactly `src/main.rs:68-72`: the DMA-BUF and arena knobs are now unconditional (`:61-62`) and only `WEBKIT_DISABLE_COMPOSITING_MODE` is behind the pref, read before the first window. The quoted 60-88 MB → 62-66 MB band is the two-state comparison from `src/main.rs:54-55`, not a stale pre-refactor number. No copy anywhere still implies DMA-BUF is switchable.
- Three of the four behavioural switches are literally true. `Let tabs sleep` ("for 30 minutes … reloads when you come back") matches `src/app.rs:1251-1291`: `!tab.asleep && !tab.loading && now - tab.last_seen > minutes*60`, `view.destroy()`, and `ensure_view` reloads on select. `Open where you left off` matches `src/app.rs:251` + `:1688`, including the `--fresh` exception (`skip_restore`). `Keep hidden things hidden` matches `src/curtain.rs:16`: `css_for` returns empty when the pref is off, so switching it off really does reveal, and the toggle reloads (`src/panels.rs:573`).
- The search engine dropdown is not decoration: five real engines (`src/store.rs:156`), real endpoints (`:144-153`), and a non-URL query reaches `search_url` through `plain_target` (`src/app.rs:2556`). Choice persists (`src/panels.rs:675-679`).
- The `THIS PAGE` group behaves: subtitle is host-accurate ("Nothing hidden on 127.0.0.1" in the shot), `set_sensitive(count > 0)` (`src/panels.rs:721`), and the empty-host cases have their own sentences (`hidden_sentence`, `:903`). Appearance's chosen chip is unmistakable in both looks: white "Light" on `#171717` and black bold "Dark" on white, from `src/css.rs:233-234`; choosing one applies at once via `switch_look` (`src/app.rs:382`).

## What costs points

- **The card is taller than the window and the last row is unreachable.** `Settings` is the only panel handed to the body without a scroller (`src/panels.rs:745`), so `measure_content` (`:264`) returns the plain preferred height and `wanted.clamp(200, 640)` (`:174`) is then ignored by the child's own request inside the `gtk::Fixed` layer (`src/layer.rs:32`). In `05-panel-settings.png` the card fill `srgb(239,239,239)` at x=1000 runs from y=141 to y=916 with no bottom border, and the side border at x=526 is `srgb(148,148,146)` at every sampled y from 690 to 916 — the card leaves the window. Every other panel ends visibly: `05-panel-shortcuts.png` bottom border at y≈779-780, `05-panel-history.png` at y≈692. The `about` line at `src/panels.rs:734` ("Search {} · WebKitGTK · Rust · this machine only") therefore never appears in either look, and the "Hidden here" row sits 7px from the clip. There is no scrollbar to reach it (the `scrollbar` rules at `src/css.rs:251-254` have nothing to attach to). Smallest fix: `self.body.add(&self.body_scroll(&list))` at `:745`, which also makes `measure_content` measure the scroller. Costs 0.9.
- **"Darken pages that stay white" is dead in Light and its subtitle is false.** `src/app.rs:1414` gates the whole effect on `darken_pages && self.dark()`, so with Appearance=Light the switch turns on, saves `true`, and paints nothing — a control that reports success and does nothing. And the sheet (`:1415-1421`) forces `html { background:#0b0b0b } … p, span, div, li, td, th, h1..h6 { color:#ededed }` on every page, including sites that already ship a dark theme and sites that honour `prefers-color-scheme: dark`; there is no detection anywhere (`grep -rn "prefers-color-scheme" src/` returns only that same stylesheet). So "Sites that do not offer a dark page get one from the browser" describes code that does not exist. Smallest fix: either apply the sheet whenever `darken_pages` is on (drop `&& self.dark()`) or disable the row with a note when the look is Light, and change the subtitle to "Every page gets the browser's dark ground and ink, including sites that are already dark." Costs 0.8.
- **"Show the bookmarks bar" cannot hide the bar.** `src/app.rs:641`: `(items, prefs.show_bookmarks_bar || !state.bookmarks.is_empty())`. With any bookmark kept, the switch is a no-op in both directions, and `render_bmarks` (`:672-678`) hides the bar only when the set is empty — which is the case where there is nothing to show anyway. Subtitle ("Shown on its own as soon as you keep something") documents the override instead of the switch working. Smallest fix: `prefs.show_bookmarks_bar && !state.bookmarks.is_empty()`, and drop the second clause of the subtitle. Costs 0.5.
- **A disabled control looks fully enabled.** `src/css.rs` has no `button:disabled` rule (`:136` styles only `button.mark:disabled`), so the insensitive "Show them" keeps its solid chip. Measured: chip fill `srgb(255,255,255)` at x=1110-1185,y=884-900 in `05-panel-settings.png`, label darkest pixel 0.0902 and mean 0.857 versus 0.954 for enabled `.act` labels in the same panel; dark look has the same problem (fill `srgb(58,58,58)` on a `srgb(26,26,26)` panel, label min 0.2275). Smallest fix: `.panel button.act:disabled { opacity: 0.35; }` (one line, mirroring `:136`). Costs 0.4.
- **Two stored preferences a real browser ships have no control at all.** `prefs.sidebar` gates the entire tab column (`src/ui.rs:81` `set_size_request(if prefs.sidebar { side_width } else { 0 }, -1)`) and is unreachable from any surface (`grep -rn "sidebar" src/panels.rs` hits only a comment at `:692`). `prefs.sleep_minutes` is rendered into a sentence at `src/panels.rs:580` and cannot be changed, so the "30 minutes" is a fact the reader can neither alter nor match to a real browser's 5-120 minute range. Smallest fix: a `Show the tabs` switch beside `Roomy tabs`, and make the sleep row a `ComboBoxText` of 5/15/30/60 that rewrites the sentence. Costs 0.4.
- **Grouping puts startup and tab behaviour in the wrong heads.** `Let tabs sleep` sits under `BROWSING` although it is a tab-memory behaviour, `Open where you left off` sits under `TABS` although it is startup, and `Use less memory` sits under `BROWSING` although it only takes effect at launch. A reader looking for "what happens when I start the browser" has to read three groups. Smallest fix: `GENERAL` (appearance, startup, memory) / `TABS` (sleep, roomy, darken) / `PRIVACY` (shield, curtain) / `SEARCH` / `THIS PAGE`. Costs 0.2.
- **Nothing in the suite would catch any of the above.** `build/stress-report.json` has 73 passing checks and not one opens Settings: no check names a row, asserts a subtitle string, exercises the engine dropdown, or asserts the card fits the window. The `SHEET` tests (`src/keys.rs:308+`) prove `Ctrl ,` opens the panel but nothing proves a control inside it is reachable. Smallest fix: one check that opens Settings, asserts every `setting_switch` title is present, and asserts `card.y + card.height <= window.height` in both looks. Costs 0.2.
- **Keyboard focus has no styling of its own.** `src/css.rs` carries `:focus` rules only for the omni field (`:184-188`); nothing styles focus on `.panel button`, `switch` or `combobox`, and `src/panels.rs:143` is the panel's only `grab_focus`. Whether GTK's theme default survives the `.act` background/border overrides cannot be settled without running the app (out of scope here), and no screenshot shows a focused control. Smallest fix: an explicit `.panel button:focus-visible, .panel switch:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid {fieldborder}; outline-offset: 2px; }`. Costs 0.2.

## The three changes that would raise this score most

1. Give Settings the same scroller every other panel has, so the card ends on screen and the version line is reachable: `self.body.add(&self.body_scroll(&list))` at `src/panels.rs:745`, then add a stress check that `card_y + card_height <= window_height` for Settings in both looks.
2. Stop the darken switch lying: drop `&& self.dark()` at `src/app.rs:1414` (or disable the row when the look is Light) and rewrite `src/panels.rs:621` to "Every page gets the browser's dark ground and ink, including sites that are already dark."
3. Fix the two controls that claim more than they do: `prefs.show_bookmarks_bar && !state.bookmarks.is_empty()` at `src/app.rs:641`, and a `:disabled` rule (`.panel button.act:disabled { opacity: 0.35; }`) so "Show them" stops looking clickable when there is nothing hidden.
