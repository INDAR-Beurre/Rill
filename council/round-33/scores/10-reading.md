## What is right

- The junk strip does its job. The `/long` fixture carries a cookie banner and a newsletter overlay (`tools/fixtures.py:19-26`), and neither appears anywhere in `07-reading.png`; the page comes back as one heading, one origin line and 399 paragraphs, which is the whole point of the mode.
- The type is really being set, not merely recoloured. Measured body band tops at y=243, 274, 305, 336 give a 30..31px pitch against `19px/1.62 = 30.78px`, paragraph pitch is 51px against `margin: 0 0 1.05em`, and the h1 ink is 183x32px against `34px` with `-0.012em` tracking (`assets/reader.js:65-70`). No accidental line-height.
- Escape ordering is correct and documented. The reader is the last arm of the chain (`src/keys.rs:226-229`), so Escape closes the omnibox, then find, then a panel, before it takes the article away, and the sheet row at `src/keys.rs:178` says "again to leave" rather than pretending the toggle is a one-way door.
- The origin line is there: `127.0.0.1:8099` in 12px muted at y=193..201, x=537..614 (`assets/reader.js:78,116`). Cheap, and most home-grown readers forget it.

## What costs points

- **Leaving reading mode does not put the page back.** `assets/reader.js:5-14` restores `document.body.innerHTML` and clears the restore string, nothing else; `document.head.querySelectorAll('[data-search-reader]').forEach(n => n.remove())` exists only at `:60`, inside the enter path. The injected sheet sets `html, body { ... !important }` (`:64`) and `body { font: 19px/1.62 ... !important; max-width: 720px !important }` (`:65`), so a page whose own body is 16px/1.5 in a 740px column (`tools/fixtures.py:30-32`) comes back at 19px in a 720px column, and the comment at `:96-101` ("leaving reader mode puts the page back exactly as it was") is false on exactly that point. Enter also does `scrollTo(0, 0)` (`:123`) and exit never scrolls back, so leaving from paragraph 380 lands the reader at paragraph 1. Smallest fix: add the style removal to the exit branch and stash/restore `window.scrollY`. Costs 0.9.
- **The exit pill is not one of the browser's controls, and it is not the pair the code calls it.** Light shot: pill x=1344..1481, y=38..64, so 138x27px, 1px `#EAEAEA` border (1.20:1 on `#FFFFFF`), radius 6px, label 114x12px in `12px system-ui`. Find bar in `06-find.png`: x=1162..1487, y=15..48, so 326x34px, a `fieldborder` edge that the repo measures at 3.95:1 on white (`src/css.rs:44-53`), radius 9px, label in `FONT` at `small`. `src/find.rs:9-11` and `assets/reader.js:88-92` both state the two sit on the same inset and top; the screen shows right edges 1487 versus 1481, tops 15 versus 38, radii 9 versus 6, and an edge contrast of 3.95 versus 1.20. Dark is worse: the pill's border renders `#242424` on `#0B0B0B` (1.27:1) while the find bar renders `#6E6E6E` at the same corner of the same window. The reader's `rule` and `muted` are hand-written hexes at `assets/reader.js:56-58`, so they sit outside `src/palette.rs` and below both floors the browser asserts for its own lines: hairline >= 1.5:1 (`src/palette.rs:240-241`) and field edge >= 3.0:1 (`src/css.rs:505-518`). Smallest fix: pass `rule`, `muted` and a field-edge colour out of `src/reader.rs` beside the palette, give the pill `border-radius: 9px` and the `FONT` stack, and drive its inset from the same `PAGE_INSET`/`PAGE_TOP` the find bar is placed with. Costs 0.7.
- **The `reading` flag has no owner.** `src/app.rs:1803` flips a `Cell<bool>`, and it is the only writer in the tree; `reload` (`src/app.rs:1315-1322`), the load-state handler (`src/web.rs:172-193`), navigation, tab switch and tab close never clear it. After a reload the page is an ordinary article while the flag still says reading, so the menu offers "Leave Reading Mode" (`src/app.rs:1949-1950`) on a page that is not in it, and Escape runs `app.reader()` (`src/keys.rs:226-229`), which sets the flag false and then enters reading mode on a page that never had it. Both the shot script (`tools/shots.sh:385`) and the suite (`tools/stress.py:1012`) reload with the flag left true, so nothing catches it. Smallest fix: clear it in `reload`, in `navigate`, in `close_tab` and on `LoadEvent::Started`, and have `reader()` ask the page (`window.__searchReaderOn`) instead of trusting the flag. Costs 0.5.
- **Dark mode is decided by a regex on a colour string.** `assets/reader.js:55` is `const dark = /#0B0B0B/i.test(palette);` against the string built at `src/reader.rs:10-11`. Change either hex and the reader keeps a `#FFFFFF` ground, an `#EAEAEA` rule and `#6B6B6B` muted on a dark page, with no error. Smallest fix: pass `{"dark": true}` or the palette struct itself instead of a CSS string. Costs 0.3.
- **The measure is not the measure it says.** The ink column is x=537..1187 and the longest line is 651px of 19px text, which is 64 characters (`Paragraph 1. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The quick` on the first line). `#__search_reader { max-width: 660px; padding: 72px 28px 120px }` (`assets/reader.js:67`) is content-box, so the 28px padding never narrows the text as written, and the extra width buys nothing on a page whose own column was 714..719px (`06-find.png` lines end at x=1207, 1211, 1215). Smallest fix: `box-sizing: border-box` on `#__search_reader`, which leaves the 604px the padding was written for. Costs 0.3.
- **The reader's type stack names faces that do not exist here, and skips the two the browser's own constant adds.** `assets/reader.js:65` starts at `"SF Pro Text", Inter`, both of which `fc-match` resolves to Noto Sans on this machine, while `src/palette.rs:135` `FONT` carries `"SF Pro"` and `"Segoe UI"` as well. So chrome and article can land on different faces from the same intent, and `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased` on a Linux text run is a Mac habit with no effect. Smallest fix: use `FONT` for the reader body too, expanded from `src/palette.rs:135` at build time the way `src/css.rs` already does. Costs 0.2.
- **Nothing in the chrome says reading mode is on.** Grep finds no reader state outside the menu row (`src/app.rs:1949-1950`) and the key sheet (`src/keys.rs:178`): no tab marker, no omnibox affordance. The shot makes it plain, because the sidebar carries two rows both titled "A long read" (`tools/shots.sh:378-380` opens the same page twice) and neither row indicates which one is in the mode. Smallest fix: a reader glyph in the live tab row from a per-tab flag, drawn where tabs already draw their close button. Costs 0.3.
- **The unreadable-page message can never fire, and leaving reports success.** `assets/reader.js:37-38` falls back to `document.body` and returns false only when `best` is null, so `src/reader.rs:19-22`'s `"Nothing to read on this page"` is unreachable on any real page, and the same `return true` covers entering and leaving. Smallest fix: return `"entered"`, `"left"` or `"empty"` and branch in `src/reader.rs:19`. Costs 0.2.
- **The only way out has no focus ring.** `all: unset` at `assets/reader.js:93` removes WebKit's default ring, and the injected sheet (`:63-79`) has no `:focus` or `:focus-visible` rule at all (grep for `focus` in `assets/reader.js` returns 0). A keyboard reader who tabs to the exit button sees nothing change. Smallest fix: `#__search_reader_exit:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid ${muted}; outline-offset: 2px; }`. Costs 0.2.
- **None of it is proven on real markup.** Every shot and every assertion uses `/long`, 399 plain paragraphs (`tools/fixtures.py:83-88`): no image, figure, caption, blockquote, code block, h2 or link. The rules for those exist at `assets/reader.js:71-77` and nothing renders or tests them (grep for `data-search-reader` or `__searchReaderRestore` under `tools/` returns no hits). Smallest fix: add a `/rich` fixture with a figure and figcaption, a blockquote, a `pre`, an h2 and an inline link, and extend `tools/stress.py:995` to assert the figure's `max-width` and the blockquote's left border. Costs 0.2.

## The three changes that would raise this score most

1. Build the exit pill out of the browser's own field: `fieldborder`-class edge at 3:1 or better, `border-radius: 9px`, the `FONT` stack at `small`, and an inset taken from the same `PAGE_INSET`/`PAGE_TOP` that `src/find.rs:12-13` places the find bar with, so the two edges land on each other (they are 6px and 23px apart today).
2. Make leaving reading mode a real restore and a real state: remove `style[data-search-reader]` on exit, restore scroll, and clear `reading` on reload, navigate and tab close, so Escape and the menu stop acting on a page that is not in the mode.
3. Take the reader's type and rules from `src/palette.rs` instead of the hexes at `assets/reader.js:56-58`, pass dark as a boolean rather than regex-matching `#0B0B0B`, and put `box-sizing: border-box` on `#__search_reader` so the 660px max-width and 28px padding give the 604px measure they describe.
