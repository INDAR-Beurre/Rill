# The dark appearance
score: 6.0/10

## What I looked at
`council/round-33-dark/*.png` all fifteen states, each against its light twin in `council/round-33/`, measured with `magick ... -format "%[pixel:p{X,Y}]"` and 1-row/1-column scans: `01-default` (0,27) and (700,10); `02-pins` y=88/95/112 tiles, y=190 live row, x=231 divider; `03-tabs` page band (490,95,200,30); `04-omni` (300,300), (600,300), (900,850); `04b-omni-picked` x=800 y=215..320 picked row; `05-panel-settings` (300,450), (540,300), x=800 y=700..880, band (556,786,300,16), segmented pill y=268; `05-panel-history` (380,620,760,300); `05-panel-shortcuts/-bookmarks/-downloads/-extensions` page corners (260,80)/(1450,880); `06-find` band (505,215,400,30); `07-reading` (525,145,540,16) and (1326,20,150,18); `08-empty` (700,492) field, (1000,486) key chip; `09-missing` (830,512) button.

Source: `src/palette.rs:97-124,158-268`, `src/css.rs:24-62,144,176,230-238,260-326,347-389,481`, `src/ui.rs:680-688`, `src/app.rs:398,417,1509-1520,2136-2141,2575-2590`, `src/web.rs:136`, `src/reader.rs:11-13`, `assets/reader.js:56-58,94`, `src/panels.rs:68,190,224-231`, `tools/shots.sh:142,203`.

The question: is this a designed dark theme or a light theme with the colours inverted? It is mostly designed — `quiet` (`src/css.rs:24-62`) and `is_dark()` (22 branches in `src/css.rs`) carry measured values, not sign-flipped ones — but three of the visible surfaces are still literally the light theme showing through, and one state is unreadable.

## What is right
- The text ramp is dark-native, not flipped: `src/palette.rs:104-115` plus `quiet` (`src/css.rs:24-62`) give muted #A6A6A6 and ROW_FG #B4B4B4, measured 8.09:1 and 9.49:1 on ground #0B0B0B, 7.15:1 on the panel fill #1A1A1A, 7.20:1 on the row hover #272727, 4.56:1 for FAINT #A4A4A4 on the wash #3A3A3A. No chrome text I could find is under 4.5:1 on its own ground.
- Dark has its own danger and control values rather than the light ones: `src/palette.rs:119` `danger: pick(pair(Rgb(0xC0,0x39,0x2B), Rgb(0xD2,0x50,0x41)))`, and `src/css.rs:370` knob #EDEDED with the ratio in a comment; measured off-switch track #787878 vs knob #EDEDED = 3.77:1 at `05-panel-settings` (1180,357)/(1182,360).
- The one place the light theme needs a chip separated from the row wash, dark separates it by going the other way rather than by copying the white chip: `src/css.rs:230` `.row.live .tile {{ background-color: {ground} }}`, so the live tile is #0B0B0B inside the live row #3C3C3C, measured at `02-pins` (26,200) vs (16,190) = 1.78:1 (above the 1.2 floor `src/palette.rs:222` asserts), with the letter at #A6A6A6 on that tile = 8.09:1.
- Reading mode is a real dark page, not a filter: `src/reader.rs:12` `background:#0B0B0B;color:#EDEDED`, `assets/reader.js:56-58` rule #242424 / muted #8A8A8A / ground #0B0B0B; measured `07-reading` (525,145,540,16) glyphs at #EDEDED and byline at 5.7:1.

## What costs points
- **The window's cut top-left corner is still light-theme grey.** `src/app.rs:2587` `context.set_source_rgb(234.0 / 255.0, 234.0 / 255.0, 233.0 / 255.0)` hard-codes the light band colour, so every dark state has a #EAEAE9 wedge against a #0A0A0A band: measured `01-default` (0,27) = `srgb(234,234,233)` vs (700,10) = `srgb(10,10,10)`, **16.46:1**, identical in all fifteen files. This is the single clearest sign of an inverted light theme, in the most prominent corner of the window. Fix: give `cut_top_left` the palette and fill with `palette.desk()`; costs 0.9.
- **Two states render the page's own dark text on the browser's dark canvas: 1.22:1.** `src/web.rs:136` (and `src/app.rs:398`, `src/app.rs:417`) set the WebKit background to `(0.043, 0.043, 0.043)` whenever the frame is dark, whatever `darken_pages` says; a page with a transparent canvas keeps its own `color:#222` (`tools/fixtures.py:28-33`) and lands on #0B0B0B. Measured `06-find` band (505,215,400,30): ground `#0B0B0B`, glyphs `#212121` → **1.22:1**; `03-tabs` (490,160,300,40) the same, while the light twin of the same region is 15.91:1. In `06-find` the only readable thing on the page is the find highlight (255,255,0). Fix: only force the dark canvas when `darken_pages` is on, or apply the `tone_for` sheet (`src/app.rs:1509-1520`) whenever the frame is dark; costs 1.2.
- **Every panel floats on a pure white page: there is no dark scrim behind a panel.** `src/app.rs:2136-2141` fills the scrim rectangle only `if self.omni.visible.get()`, so the panel layer's own scrim (`src/panels.rs:65,101`) never lands in the picture and the card's `box-shadow: 0 18px 48px rgba(0,0,0,0.16)` (`src/css.rs:295`) falls on white. Measured `05-panel-settings` (300,450) = `srgb(255,255,255)`, card (540,300) = `srgb(26,26,26)`, shadow ring 229→253 across x=800 y=780..876; the light twin's page is the identical (300,450) = 255. A #1A1A1A card on white is 17.4:1, so it is legible, but it reads as dark chrome dropped onto an untouched light theme. Fix: fill that rectangle when `panels.open.is_some()` as well; costs 0.6, and it is the same edit class as the `06-find`/`03-tabs` filing because it is the same missing scrim path.
- **Panel content escapes the card and lands as light-theme text on white.** `05-panel-settings` band (556,786,300,16) (the "Roomy tabs" title the card cuts off) has its brightest text pixel at **#ACACAC on #F1F1F1 = 1.98:1**, where the light twin's same band is #171717 = 11.89:1. The rows are drawn outside the card's rect with no clip (the card is 680x~640, `src/panels.rs:190` clamps to 640). Fix: clip the card's body to its rounded rect; costs ~0.3.
- **The danger colour fails on the ground it is actually used on.** `src/css.rs:314` `.panel button.act.quiet:hover label {{ color: {danger} }}` paints #D25041 on the chip fill #3A3A3A (`src/css.rs:380`), measured **2.69:1**, and on the hover #272727 it is 3.53:1. `src/palette.rs:158-180` only asserts danger against `p.ground` and the sidebar (4.65:1), so the test cannot see it. Fix: add a dark `danger_on_chip` (#E8735F is 4.6:1 on #3A3A3A) and assert that pair; costs ~0.25.
- **The address field's outline misses the 3:1 it claims over the scrim.** `src/css.rs:384-387` says "the dark pair is 3.05:1 on the dark ground", and FIELD_BORDER #6E6E6E is 3.86:1 on #0B0B0B — but with the field up the ground behind it is the composited scrim: `04-omni` fill (600,300) = `srgb(26,26,26)`, page (300,300) = `srgb(44,44,44)`, border #6E6E6E → **2.74:1**, and the border is only 1.25:1 against its own fill. Fix: measure and pick against the scrim (#8A8A8A ≈ 3.4:1 on #2C); costs ~0.2.
- **The reading-mode exit chip's outline is invisible.** `assets/reader.js:94` `border: 1px solid ${rule}` with `rule = '#242424'` (`assets/reader.js:56`) on the dark ground #0B0B0B = **1.27:1**; measured `07-reading` (1326,20,150,18) contains nothing lighter than #242424, so the chip reads as loose grey text at top right. Same token is the `pre/code` fill. Fix: a separate #4A4A4A boundary token; costs ~0.15.
- **The dark theme has no accent at all.** Grepping `palette.rs` and `css.rs` for `accent|#007AFF|#0A84FF` returns nothing: every surface, chip, pill and the chosen-switch state are greyscale, and the only chromatic dark values are danger and the find highlight. The single coloured link in the product is a literal outside the theme, `src/app.rs:1514` `a, a:visited {{ color: #9ecbff !important; }}`, so a page's links do not follow the palette. Fix: one `accent` token in `Palette` used by that sheet and by the chosen/focused state; costs ~0.2.
- **The band and the sidebar step 1.0059:1.** `src/css.rs:368` `desk = "#0A0A0A"`, `src/palette.rs:115` card #0B0B0B behind the sidebar (`side_bg()`): measured (700,10) = 10 and (30,30) = 11. In light the same pair is 234 vs 255 and the difference is the whole point of the chamfer. The five surfaces do not collapse — ground #0B0B0B, panel/field #1A1A1A, hover #272727, tile #2A2A2A, wash #3A3A3A are 1.13/1.32/1.37/1.73:1 steps — but the outermost one is a no-op. Fix: #101010 for the dark desk; costs 0.2.

## The three changes that would raise this score most
1. Stop putting the browser's dark canvas under a page's own dark text: gate the `set_background_color` dark value at `src/web.rs:136`, `src/app.rs:398` and `src/app.rs:417` on `darken_pages` (or apply the `tone_for` sheet whenever the frame is dark), so `03-tabs` and `06-find` stop showing body copy at 1.22:1.
2. Make `cut_top_left` (`src/app.rs:2575-2587`) take the palette and fill the corner with the dark band colour instead of the literal 234/233, deleting the 16.46:1 light-grey wedge that is present in all fifteen dark states.
3. Fill the scrim for panels the way it is filled for the field (`src/app.rs:2136-2141`, condition on `panels.open`), and clip the card body, so the six panel states stop being a #1A1A1A card on a #FFFFFF page with 1.98:1 overflow text beneath it.
