## What is right

- The switch is a real switch, not a checkbox in a pill: 34x20 track, 10px radius, 16px knob with 2px margin, checked = ink fill with the knob in the field's own colour (`src/css.rs:262-265`). Measured light ON at x1149-1188, y343-362: track `#171717`, knob white; dark ON: track `#EDEDED`, knob `#1A1A1A`.
- The off state is guaranteed legible by a test rather than by eye: `src/css.rs:518-521` asserts `track >= 3.0`, and the light off track measures `#828280` on the `#EFEFEF` panel and `#787878` on the `#1A1A1A` dark panel.
- Copy states mechanism and timing honestly where it could have hidden them: "Use less memory" says it "Applies the next time the browser opens" (`src/panels.rs:714`), and that is exactly what the code does, reading the pref into the environment before the first window (`src/main.rs:66-72`).
- Rows have one rhythm and it holds: 7px above and below every row (`src/panels.rs:897-898`), 2px between title and subtitle, title `.setting` at body size in ink and subtitle `.settingdesc` at small size in muted (`src/css.rs:345`, `:348`), group heads at 11px bold with 0.07em tracking (`src/css.rs:346`). Measured pitch is even across eleven rows, and the switch column never drifts: every track's right edge lands on x=1188, 17px inside the card's border at x=1205.

## What costs points

**1. The settings list is painted past the bottom of its own card, and in dark mode the spilled rows are unreadable.** Defect: the card's border closes at y=776-777, and four rows plus a group head then continue to the window edge on the page ground instead of the card's. Evidence: at x=1000 the card border is `srgb(148,148,146)` at y=779 (light) and `srgb(110,110,110)` (dark); below it the ground is a light gradient from `srgb(229)` at y=780 to `srgb(254)` at y=880 in *both* looks, while the card fill is `srgb(239)` light and `srgb(26)` dark. The rows landing on that light ground are "Roomy tabs" (title y~786, subtitle y~800), "Open where you left off" (y~833, y~851), the "SEARCH" head (y~886) and the Search-engine `ComboBoxText`, which is cut off by the window bottom. In light those titles stay `#171717` (crop `96x13+541+786` min=23) and read; in dark the same crop is min=166 / max=243, mean 235, which is dark-mode ink `#EDEDED` on a `#E9E9E9` ground: about 1.05:1. The spilled switches for those rows sit on the same light ground with dark-mode fills, so the last screen of Settings in the dark look is an empty-looking band. Smallest fix: give the card's body the card's own height so the scroller clips it (`src/panels.rs:251`, already written and uncommitted in this tree: `self.body.set_size_request(-1, (card_height - chrome).max(1));`), and add one stress check that the last row's bottom is `<=` the card's bottom in both looks. Cost: 1.4.
**2. A panel in the dark look does not dim the page it covers.** Defect: with Settings open in dark, the page outside the card is `srgb(255,255,255)` at (400,700), (1430,60) and (1400,300), the same as the light look, while round 32's dark panel shot measured `srgb(44,44,44)` there. Evidence: the capture path fills a scrim rectangle only for the address field, `src/app.rs:2233` `if self.omni.visible.get()`, and the panel's own scrim is a `GtkDrawingArea` whose draw GTK drops at capture time (the comment at `src/app.rs:2228-2232` says so). `council/round-33-dark/04-omni.png` shows the dimmed page because the field's scrim is filled; all six panel shots in that directory show none. Smallest fix: extend that block to `if self.omni.visible.get() || self.panels.borrow().open.is_some()` and fill the same rectangle from the column. Cost: 0.7.
**3. "Appearance" is three loose chips with a dead class name, styled unlike every other section head.** Defect: `crate::ui::class(&buttons, "segmented")` at `src/panels.rs:649`, and `segmented` appears nowhere in `src/css.rs` (0 matches in the whole file). Rendered as three `button.act` chips with 2px gaps: measured at y=262, chip one x543-597, chip two x600-650, chip three x654-720, each 40px tall. They read as three independent buttons, not one control, and the chosen one is a solid ink slab. The label above them is `.setting` in title case (`src/panels.rs:644`) while the four other heads are `.group` in caps, so the first section of the panel is the only one shaped differently. Smallest fix: one rule block for `.panel .segmented` (shared `wash` track, 1px `hairline` inset, chosen chip on `card` with `ink` label) and change that one label's class to `group` with the text "APPEARANCE". Cost: 0.6.
**4. Two stored preferences have no control, and one number on screen cannot be changed.** Defect: `prefs.sleep_minutes` (`src/store.rs:40`, default 30 at `:100`) is interpolated into a sentence, "A tab you have not looked at for {} minutes" (`src/panels.rs:687-693`), with no stepper anywhere; `prefs.sidebar` (`src/store.rs:34`) gates the tab column at `src/ui.rs:94` and appears in `src/panels.rs` only in a comment; `src/app.rs:1853` `pub fn zoom()` exists behind Ctrl +/-/0 but has no row and is not persisted. Evidence: `grep -n "sidebar\|sleep_minutes" src/panels.rs` returns no widget construction. Smallest fix: a `TABS` row using the same `setting_switch` helper wired to `prefs.sidebar`, and a 15/30/60 `ComboBoxText` on the sleep row. Cost: 0.5.
**5. The "Use less memory" row sells a comparison the shipped build cannot reproduce.** Defect: the subtitle quotes "from 60-88 MB to a steadier 62-66 MB" (`src/panels.rs:714`); those are two different stages of the `src/main.rs:54-56` table, and the middle stage's knobs (DMA-BUF off, `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2`) are now unconditional at `src/main.rs:61-62`. `README.md:22-26` admits the 62-66 figure was "taken before two of those knobs became unconditional". The current suite measures one idle tab at `pss_mb 179.2`, `anon_mb 63.5`, `rss_mb 280.2`, `processes 3`. Smallest fix: drop the historical band and state what the switch does now, "turns off the engine's compositing path; applies at the next launch". Cost: 0.4.
**6. Nothing paints focus, and nothing tests this panel.** Defect: `grep -n ":focus" src/css.rs` returns only `src/css.rs:293` (`.omni entry:focus`), so keyboard focus on a switch or chip is invisible, and the capture grabs focus on the card itself (`src/panels.rs:191-195` in the working tree). `build/stress-report.json` has 79 checks and none of them opens Settings: the panel-related names are `panels open and close`, `a panel filters as you type`, `the Shortcuts panel lists keys that work`, `tab switches stay quick`. Smallest fix: `button:focus, switch:focus { box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px {fieldborder}; }` and one stress check that opens Settings in both looks and asserts row count and card fit. Cost: 0.4.

## The three changes that would raise this score most

1. Land the body height bound so the card clips its content, then re-shoot: `src/panels.rs:251`, `self.body.set_size_request(-1, (card_height - chrome).max(1));`. The uncommitted tree already has it; the shipped picture still shows four rows and the engine dropdown painted outside the card, and in the dark look those rows are white-on-white at about 1.05:1. A stress check asserting last row bottom <= card bottom in both looks keeps it landed.
2. Paint the panel scrim in the capture path the way the field's is painted: `src/app.rs:2233` currently gates the fill on `self.omni.visible.get()`. Every dark panel shot otherwise shows a white page behind a dark card, so the panel reads as a light-mode window with dark chrome, and the overflow from change 1 has nowhere dark to hide.
3. Give Appearance a real segmented control and make its heading a group head: one `.panel .segmented` rule (shared track, chosen chip raised, no 2px gaps) plus `group`/caps on the label. As shipped it is three separate chips of 40px with a dead class name, which is the first thing a picky reviewer sees in the first section of the panel.
