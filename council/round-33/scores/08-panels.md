## What costs points

- **History rows paint outside their own card, and in dark they paint unreadable on white.** In `council/round-33/05-panel-history.png` the card's border line sits at y=710 (srgb(148,148,146) at x=530/700/1200, card x=526..1205) yet scanline ink continues at y=712 (105 px), 718 (113 px), then a whole seventh row at y=742..772 with its "Open" and "Remove" chips, on bare page srgb(254,254,254) with only the card's shadow under it; y=780..802 is clean, so this is drawn ink, not a template. Dark is worse: `council/round-33-dark/05-panel-history.png` has the same y=710 border and the spilled row is dark-theme ink on a white page — "Search" band x 590..660, y 752..772 reads min 166 on max 248 (3.28:1 at best, 2.07:1 for the quiet label), and the spilled "Remove" chip x 1145..1195, y 755..770 sits at min 237 on max 251, i.e. gone. Mechanism: `src/layer.rs` `place()` is `set_size_request` + `Fixed::move_`, which never clips, so a body taller than the card's allocation simply paints past the border. Smallest fix: propagate the card's height bound to `self.body` before layout (the working tree already has this at `src/panels.rs:240-251`, `self.body.set_size_request(-1, (card_height - chrome).max(1))`; the shots predate it) and make `measure_content` (`src/panels.rs:294`) use the same width the body is laid out at, `card_width - 32` not `- 40`. Cost 0.7.
- **No scrim behind any panel: the page stays at full strength under a "modal", and in dark a #1A1A1A card floats on a #FFFFFF page.** Probing `01-default.png` → `05-panel-bookmarks.png`: (600,300) 255→251, (900,330) 255→244, (1200,500) 255→239 — that ramp is the card's own `box-shadow: 0 18px 48px` (`src/css.rs:295`), not a veil; the page's black "Start a project" pill at (700,622) is 17 → 16, still full strength, and the corners (300,200), (300,850), (1300,850) do not move at all in either theme. `04-omni.png` shows the intended value: (300,400) = (900,700) = (1350,300) = srgb(44,44,44). The scrim widget exists (`src/panels.rs:65`, placed at `:225`) but the capture path paints it only for the omnibox: `src/app.rs:2233` `if self.omni.visible.get() {` … `context.fill();` — so every panel shot in the round is scrimless, and the comment at `src/app.rs:2228-2232` (GTK drops a draw on a child whose allocation is pending) says the live first frame after Ctrl Y is at the same risk. Smallest fix: `if self.omni.visible.get() || self.panels.borrow().open.is_some()` with `column` taken from the panel's placement. Cost 0.6.
- **Empty panels are 240px slabs with one line off the grid.** `05-panel-bookmarks.png` and `05-panel-downloads.png` both have their card border at x=700, y=340 and y=579 — exactly the `.max(240)` floor at `src/panels.rs:229` — with a single faint line at y=448..462 and ~117px of empty card below it, while the page's own controls stay legible around it. The two lines centre 30px apart (bookmarks ink x 830..1183, centre 1006; downloads x 768..1184, centre 976) and neither starts on the 542 body inset that `05-panel-extensions.png` uses (list starts x=554). Cause: `src/panels.rs:942` `set_justify(gtk::Justification::Center)` with no `set_halign`, inside a stretched body. Smallest fix: drop the 240 floor for a body with no rows (clamp to `chrome + 96`) and set `label.set_halign(gtk::Align::Start)` with a body-inset margin. Cost 0.3.
- **"Clear History" is one click from irrecoverable, and sits 30px from the filter field.** `src/panels.rs:381-395`: the `act` chip (x≈1089..1186, y≈294..321 in the light shot) runs `state.history.clear(); Records::save_history(&[]);` with no confirm and no undo, while the filter entry begins at y=332. A picky reviewer files this; a user with 7 pages and a slipped click loses them. Smallest fix: the chip becomes "Clear History" → inline "Sure?" state for 3s (or an 8-second undo toast via `flash_toast`). Cost 0.2.
- **No keyboard path through any panel list.** `grep -n "connect_key_press_event" src/panels.rs` returns nothing and the only focus call is `src/panels.rs:191` `self.card.set_can_focus(true)`; rows are EventBoxes with a press handler (`:1030`) and per-row actions are unreachable without a pointer. In a browser whose Shortcuts panel advertises 76 keys, Ctrl Y then Tab cannot reach a single row. Smallest fix: make the row list a focusable container with a selection index and bind Up/Down/Return/Delete in one `connect_key_press_event`. Cost 0.2.
- **Downloads gets neither a filter nor a clear-all.** `filter_field` appears only at `src/panels.rs:330` (bookmarks) and `:396` (history); the only downloads-wide control is per-row Remove at `:500`, so a folder of 40 files has no way to be searched or emptied, and `Records::save_downloads` keeps `take(200)` of them. Cost 0.1.
- **Every stress check passes while all of the above is true.** "panels open and close" measures 74ms mean over 80 opens and "a panel filters as you type" only reads back `probe["panelFilter"]` (`tools/stress.py:719-721`); nothing asserts that a row lands inside the card, that the page behind is dimmed, or that a populated Bookmarks/Downloads panel is even photographed (the manifest notes for those two are one word, "bookmarks" / "downloads"). Smallest fix: one check that crops the 40px band under the card and asserts zero sub-200 pixels. Cost 0.2.

## The three changes that would raise this score most

1. **Bound the card's height onto its body so no row can leave the border, then prove it in CI.** Keep the `src/panels.rs:240-251` propagation, make `measure_content` use the body's real width (`card_width - 32`), and add the band crop under the card as a stress assertion — that single check would have caught this round's history shot, and would have caught Settings' and Shortcuts' spill too.
2. **Paint the scrim for panels in the capture path and on the first live frame** (`src/app.rs:2233` extended to `self.panels.borrow().open.is_some()`, using the panel column). One condition turns the dark sheet from a #1A1A1A slab on a white page into a modal, and un-breaks every panel shot at once.
3. **Give the list a keyboard path and make destruction reversible** — a selection index with Up/Down/Return/Delete in `row_with` (`src/panels.rs:960-1042`), plus a confirm or undo on "Clear History" (`src/panels.rs:381`). These are the two places where the panel is not merely unfinished but unsafe to use.
