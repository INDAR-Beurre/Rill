## What is right

- The panic surface is genuinely zero in production code. `grep -rn "unwrap()\|expect(\|panic!" src/` hits only `#[cfg(test)]` bodies (first test line per file: `src/app.rs:2825`, `src/web.rs:589`, `src/panels.rs:1038`, `src/extensions.rs:996`), and all 10 `unsafe` sites carry a written reason — `src/app.rs:565`, `:681`, `:1387` each precede `unsafe { view.destroy() };` with `// SAFETY: the view was just taken out of its container and nothing else holds a reference to it in this scope`, and the shield FFI at `src/shield.rs:193, 201, 249, 283, 315, 501, 548` is documented the same way. In a GTK + WebKit codebase this is the difference between a browser that survives a spray and one that aborts.
- The tab invariant really is centralised. `state.tabs` is mutated in exactly four places, all in `src/app.rs`: insert `:467`, remove `:573`, re-push the last tab `:577`, active `:580`; nothing outside `app.rs` touches the vector (the other `state.tabs.` hits at `:790, :811, :1339, :1363` are reads). The one borrow panic this file ever had is written down as a rule where the next person will hit it: `src/app.rs:806-807` "Collected first: `update_row_title` borrows the state for writing, and a borrow held across that is how this panicked at launch."
- The persistence layer is the strongest module boundary in the tree. `src/files.rs:62` `write_private` creates with `create_new(true)` + `mode(0o600)`, `sync_all`s, then `set_permissions(... from_mode(0o600))` at `:93` before the rename, directories at `0o700` (`:120`), and `temp_path` (`:128`) mixes pid, subsecond nanos and an `AtomicU64` so two instances cannot collide. `src/store.rs` sits on top of it and swallows a corrupt file by starting over (`src/files.rs:4`), which is the right call for a preferences file.
- Cross-cutting concerns are compiled once and hashed, not recomputed per tab: the filter lists are keyed by the hash of the list bytes (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:35-40`, 14,002 rules confirmed by the report check "every filter list compiled"), and the memory-conservation machinery is a single timer (`src/app.rs:1428-1439`) whose 120-second period carries its own justification in four lines of comment rather than being a magic number.

## The three changes that would raise this score most

1. **Make the architecture document true, and let the file layout do the arguing.** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:101-108` currently asserts three things the tree contradicts (`ui.rs:377/503/538` exports the tab row; `app.rs` names 27 `gtk::` types outside the capture region, including `gtk::Menu::new()` at `:1859`; `web.rs:460` calls `panels`). Moving `TabRow`/`TabState`/`tab_row`/`loading_mark` into `src/rows.rs` and the 707-line capture walk (`src/app.rs:2073-2779`) into `src/paint.rs` makes two of the three claims true by construction and drops `app.rs` from 2,867 lines to roughly 2,000 — then the remaining paragraph is a rewrite, not an apology. Worth 1.4 of the 3.7 points off, and it is the deduction any reviewer opens with.
2. **Delete the ambient singleton: `weak()` reads `self`, not `CURRENT`.** `src/app.rs:348-350` ignores its receiver, and the file records the two bugs that produced: the controls that "did nothing at all" (`:307-311`) and the tabs that never slept (`:1424-1427`). Storing a `Weak<App>` on `App` and passing `&self` at the five in-method `current()` sites (`ensure_view` :621, `refresh_offers` :1544, `filter_panel` :1637, `open_panel` :1651, `reader` :1753) removes 16 ambient lookups across `find.rs`/`omnibox.rs`/`panels.rs`/`curtain.rs`/`bench.rs` and makes `open_panel` a method that cannot be called for the wrong app. Worth 0.6.
3. **Compile the test socket out, and assert the leak counter that already exists.** `src/bench.rs` is 718 lines and a 523-line `dispatch` in the shipped 2.9 MB binary behind one environment variable, opening a socket with no `0o600` and no peer check (`src/bench.rs:67-78`; contrast `src/files.rs:93`). Gate it on `[features] bench`, chmod the socket, and wire `orphan_views` (`src/app.rs:532`, currently called by nothing except `src/bench.rs:406`) into `tools/stress.py` after "closing every tab leaves one" as `count == 0`, with `stackChildren == 1` from the probe. Worth 0.8, and it turns "the count is worth being able to read" (`src/app.rs:531`) from a comment into a check.
