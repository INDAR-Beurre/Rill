## What is right
- The card grid is real and consistent: left and right border both at x=526 and x=1205 in all six panels (`05-panel-settings.png`, `05-panel-shortcuts.png`, `05-panel-history.png`, `05-panel-bookmarks.png`, `05-panel-extensions.png`, `05-panel-downloads.png`), head title ink at x=544, body row title ink at x=544, group head ink at x=544, so title, tabs, rows and group heads share one 18px inset from the border (`INSET` at `src/panels.rs:12` plus label border).
- Card centre: 526..1205 is 680 wide inside a 1500 window with a 232 column, so the page area is 1268 and 1268-680=588, 294 each side, and measured left gap 526-232=294 and right gap 1500-1205=295. `layout_card`'s `column + ((page - card_width)/2).max(12)` (`src/panels.rs:228`) lands on the pixel.
- Find pill and reading pill agree: `06-find.png` right border at x=1481 and `07-reading.png` exit pill right edge at x=1481, both at top 14 (`06-find.png` y=15..48, `07-reading.png` y=15..64). `src/find.rs:5-7` `PAGE_INSET=18`, `PAGE_TOP=14` and `assets/reader.js:95` `top: 14px; right: 18px` actually match; no drift.
- No overlapping furniture anywhere: the omni block (field 586..1146 x, 207..249 y) and its list (586..1145 x, 250..607 y) meet at a shared border row, and the list bottom (607) clears the window (917) by 310px. `04b-omni-picked.png` rows are identical in position to `04-omni.png` (title ink both `(628..636, 284..294)`), so the picked row's 2px left border does not shift its content.

## What costs points

1. **Settings and Shortcuts paint their last rows outside the card, with no scrollbar and no clipping.** Card bottom border is y=779 in both `05-panel-settings.png` and `05-panel-shortcuts.png`, yet ink continues to y=910 in Settings (`[780,790]`, `[797,807]`, `[830,843]`, `[850,860]`, `[883,891]`, `[915,916]` in x 540..1100) and to y=909 in Shortcuts (`[781,783]`, `[814,824]`, `[854,868]`, `[895,909]`). Those rows are painted on the bare desktop ground (255,255,255) below the card, outside its 1px frame, and the version line at `src/panels.rs:824-829` is one of them, cut by the window edge at y=917. Cause: the card height is capped at 640 by `self.wanted_height.set(wanted.clamp(200, 640))` (`src/panels.rs:190`), so `layout_card`'s `card_height` (`src/panels.rs:225`) stops at 640 while `build_settings` puts the whole list in `body_scroll` with no `set_max_content_height` and no `propagate_natural_height` (`src/panels.rs:289-294`), and `body_scroll`'s policy is `Never`/`Automatic` (`src/panels.rs:291`) so no vertical bar is drawn either. `grep -c D6D6D4` (the `scroll` slider colour, `src/css.rs:388`) over `05-panel-settings.png` = 0 and over `05-panel-shortcuts.png` = 0: there is no thumb anywhere in either card. Dark build is identical (`round-33-dark/05-panel-settings.png` ink at y=912..916). Cost: this is the single most visible layout defect in the build; it is the first thing a reviewer files.
   Smallest fix: in `build_settings` and `build_shortcuts`, replace `self.body.add(&self.body_scroll(&list));` with a scroll whose height is bounded by the body it is given (`scroll.set_min_content_height(0)` is already implicit; add `scroll.set_propagate_natural_height(false)`) and let the card's `wanted.clamp` stop at the window: change `src/panels.rs:190` to `wanted.clamp(200, 640)` measured *after* subtracting head+nav, i.e. `wanted.min(height - 80)` at layout time rather than a fixed 640. Note the content-floor is fine (`measure_content` at `src/panels.rs:280-288` already reads the inner box), it is only the clamp that is wrong.

2. **Empty-state text is centred against the card, not against the card's body, so it does not line up with anything.** `05-panel-bookmarks.png` empty line ink spans x=830..1188 at y=451..464, i.e. it ends 17px short of the card's right border (1205) and starts 304px right of the body inset (526+16=542). `05-panel-downloads.png` spans x=768..1183, a different centre again (975 vs Bookmarks 1009). Cause: `empty_state` sets only `label.set_justify(gtk::Justification::Center)` (`src/panels.rs:915`) and no `set_halign`; the label is added straight to `self.body` (`src/panels.rs:310`, `:350`, `:448`), a vertical box, so it stretches and centres the glyphs, and the string length moves the visible centre. No other text in the card behaves this way: every other row is xalign 0.0 at x=544. Cost: two panels' only content is misaligned relative to each other and to the grid; a reader sees a floating sentence.
   Smallest fix: `src/panels.rs:915`, add `label.set_halign(gtk::Align::Start);` and `label.set_valign(gtk::Align::Start);` and drop the `set_justify` line, so empty states start at the body inset like every row.

3. **The Appearance segmented control does not sit on the right-hand rail that every other control uses.**
   Measured on `05-panel-settings.png`: the Light/Dark/System group's ink and frame span x=543..709 (`y=233..266`), while the switches end at x=1188 (row 1 track bbox `1149..1188`, y=343..359), the "Show them" button ends at x=1188 (y=840..854) and the DuckDuckGo combo's interior stops at x=1188 (row 790). So the switches, the button and the combo share one right edge at 1188 and the segmented control stops 479px short of it, leaving the right half of the Appearance block empty. Cause: the segmented box at `src/panels.rs:632-649` is left-packed in the appearance box with no `set_halign` and no hexpand, so its width is just its three labels plus `padding: 5px 10px` (`src/css.rs:309`). Cost: the one control with a different visual weight (the chosen chip is a solid `ink` pill, `src/css.rs:176`) is also the only one not on the rail, which reads as a layout mistake rather than a choice.
   Smallest fix: after `crate::ui::class(&buttons, "segmented");` at `src/panels.rs:633`, add `buttons.set_halign(gtk::Align::End);`.

4. **The tab column's own insets disagree with each other and with the reference.** Live-row wash in `03-tabs.png` starts at x=10 and ends at x=221, an 11px gap to the divider at x=231; the reference's live-row wash starts at x=19 and ends at x=231, flush to the divider (`reference/reference-window.png`, rows 148..158, measured wash 19..231). The reference also has no visible gap on the right of the wash at all. Cause: `rows.set_margin_start(10); rows.set_margin_end(9)` (`src/ui.rs`, `side` construction) while the reference insets the wash 18px on the left only. Additionally the letter tile sits at x=10..69 in the live row (`03-tabs.png`, white tile bbox) which is flush with the wash edge, whereas the reference's tile sits 8px inside its wash.
   Cost: the column reads as a floating card of rows rather than rows in a column; it is the most-looked-at region after the page.
   Smallest fix: `src/ui.rs`, rows box: `set_margin_start(8)`, `set_margin_end(0)`, and give `tab_row`'s hbox `set_margin_start(9)` so the tile lands 8px inside the wash while the wash stays flush to the divider.

5. **Pin grid row pitch and the tab row pitch are not related to the 28px row height.** Pins: tile column runs at x=10..84, 86..120, 124..158 (`02-pins.png`, wash bbox 10..231 x, 60..149 y) - the first "run" is tile plus adjacent wash, so the tiles are 35px wide with 3px gaps, matching `ui.rs` `event.set_size_request(35,35)` and `column_spacing 3`. Rows: title ink bands at y=76..86, 106..116, 136..146 in `01-default.png`, i.e. pitch exactly 30, though `row_height()` returns 28 (`src/store.rs:135-141`) and `tab_row` sets `event.set_size_request(-1, row_height)` (`src/ui.rs:528`). The extra 2px comes from `rows` box `spacing 2` (`src/ui.rs`), so the nominal 28px row is drawn on a 30px pitch - a value that appears nowhere in the source. Cost: minor, but it means a "roomy tabs" 36 becomes 38, and any check that asserts row pitch 28 will not match the picture.
   Smallest fix: `src/ui.rs`, `rows` box: `widget_box(Vertical, 0)` so pitch equals `row_height`.

6. **The suggestion list's group heads do not line up with the rows under them, and the two boxes use different radii.** Field and list share their edges (both 586..1145, `src/omnibox.rs:12` `FIELD_WIDTH` 560), so the stack itself is right. But the group head "Open" ink starts at x=600 while every row title under it starts at x=628 - a 28px step between a head and the column it labels. Cause: the head gets only `set_margin_start(12)` (`src/omnibox.rs`, head block: `head.set_margin_start(12)`), while the row's hbox gets the same 12 plus an 18px mark and `spacing 10`, so heads align to the row's mark column instead of its text. Radii also disagree: `.omni { border-radius: 12px; }` (`src/css.rs:261`) over `.suggest { border-radius: 10px; }` (`src/css.rs:269`), so the list's corners are visibly tighter than the field's directly above them. Cost: two individually file-able inconsistencies on the most-used surface in the browser.
   Smallest fix: `src/omnibox.rs`, change the head to `head.set_margin_start(40);` (12 + 18 + 10) so "Open" sits over the row titles, and set `.suggest { border-radius: 12px; }` at `src/css.rs:269` to match the field.

7. **Reading mode's exit pill and the find pill do not share a height, and the reading pill is 6px taller and unframed on the right.** `07-reading.png` pill occupies y=15..64 (50px tall, border rows at 15 and 64), `06-find.png` pill y=15..48 (34px). `src/find.rs` fixes `34`, and `assets/reader.js:95` sets the reading pill by content. So when both are up (find inside reading mode) the two neighbours differ by 16px in height at the same top and right inset. Cost: small, but it is exactly the pair the code comment at `assets/reader.js:90-95` claims are matched.
   Smallest fix: `assets/reader.js:95`, add `height: 34px; box-sizing: border-box; padding: 0 10px; line-height: 32px;` alongside the existing `top: 14px; right: 18px;`.

8. **The window's top band is inconsistent with its own bottom.** `01-default.png` row 1 is `(234,234,233)` across the full width and rows 0..22 are that flat grey, then white from y=24; the reference's band is a gradient `#EDEDEC`->`#E6E6E5` (`reference/reference-window.png`, x=600, y=0..22). More importantly, `cut_top_left` (`src/app.rs:2569-2583`) rounds only the top-left corner with r=14 and paints the sliver `234/234/233`, while `src/css.rs` sets `let radius = 0;` for the window, so the other three corners are square (`01-default.png` corners (1499,0) and (0,916) are hard 90-degree joins). Cost: one rounded corner on a square window reads as a bug, not a style.
   Smallest fix: either cut all four corners in `cut_top_left` with the same r and colour, or drop the cut entirely and keep `radius = 0`; the current half-measure is the worst of both.

## The three changes that would raise this score most
1. Make `05-panel-settings` and `05-panel-shortcuts` end inside their card: their last rows (the version line at `src/panels.rs:824-829` and the last keycap rows) are painted below the card's bottom border at y=779, at y up to 916, with no scrollbar drawn (`src/panels.rs:190` clamp 640 vs `src/panels.rs:225` card height).
2. Fix `empty_state` (`src/panels.rs:915`) to `set_halign(gtk::Align::Start)` so panel empty states start at the 542 inset like every other row instead of centring at x=830 vs x=768 in two panels.
3. Put the Appearance segmented control on the same right rail as the switches (`src/panels.rs:633`, `buttons.set_halign(gtk::Align::End)`): it currently ends at x=709 while the switches, the "Show them" button and the search-engine combo all stop at 1188.
